===STYLE_BIBLE===
STYLE_ANCHOR: cinematic oil painting, painterly brushwork, rich earthy palette, dramatic chiaroscuro
GENRE: Techno-thriller / Science fiction / Adventure
SETTING: 1980s Costa Rica, Montana badlands, and Isla Nublar (fictional island off Costa Rica)
TONE: Awe and wonder mixed with escalating dread and moral unease as scientific hubris leads to catastrophic consequences
NOTES: Cinematic oil painting suits both the corporate boardrooms and high-tech labs of InGen and the mist-shrouded prehistoric island landscapes. Painterly brushwork unifies scenes ranging from sterile genetic engineering facilities to tropical jungles and dinosaur-filled vistas. The dramatic chiaroscuro emphasizes the tension between enlightenment and disaster that runs through the novel.

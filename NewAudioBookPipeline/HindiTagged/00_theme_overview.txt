# Jurassic Park — Theme Overview
Book: Jurassic Park
Author: Michael Crichton
Year: 1990
Genre: Techno-thriller / Science Fiction / Adventure

## Plot Gist
Billionaire John Hammond has used cutting-edge genetic engineering to clone dinosaurs from DNA trapped in prehistoric amber. He builds a theme park on a remote Costa Rican island, Isla Nublar, and invites experts — paleontologist Alan Grant, paleobotanist Ellie Sattler, and chaos mathematician Ian Malcolm — to inspect it before opening. But the park's systems fail catastrophically: the dinosaurs escape, the security fences lose power, and the visitors must fight for survival while corporate espionage, scientific hubris, and nature's unpredictability collide.

## Setting
1980s. Starts in Costa Rican coastal villages, Montana badlands, and San Francisco corporate offices, then moves to Isla Nublar — a volcanic island eight miles long, wreathed in fog, with tropical jungle, steam vents, and state-of-the-art genetic labs and electrified enclosures.

## Tone
Starts with creeping dread and scientific wonder, builds to escalating terror and chaos, and ends with philosophical reflection on humanity's relationship with nature. The narrator is omniscient third-person, moving between multiple character perspectives with a clinical but engaged voice.

## Hindi Tone Register
Casual Mumbai-Hindi with serious undertone in action/horror scenes. Dialogue-driven sections should feel like Bollywood thriller conversations. Scientific explanations should be clear and conversational, not textbook-like. The narrator's voice is Madhur (calm male).

## Pacing
Terse and propulsive. Action scenes are short, punchy. Scientific exposition is woven into dialogue and tour sequences. The book has a journalistic feel with short chapters/sections.

## Era Flavor
1990 — contemporary for its time. Keep the feel modern. Tech references (Cray supercomputers, CD-ROMs) should stay as-is.
